A small pricing fix, and the shops and boards now show every wallet they deal in.

- **Balance: the Featured: Fisher's Haul cache costs Fire Essence too.** Buying it used to cost only 160 Bounty Tokens; it now also asks for 24 Fire Essence.
- **New: every shop and board shows the currencies it actually deals in.** The balance at the top of a page was always Bounty Tokens. The Token Shop and the XP Exchange now show your Bounty Tokens and your Life Essence side by side, since both price offers in the pair, and the daily bounty board shows both too, because one of its contracts asks you to spend Essence. Needs MMO Skill Tree 1.6.0 or newer; on an older version the pack loads exactly as before, with the single Bounty Token balance.
- **New: the Token Shop lists its categories in a deliberate order.** Its catalog reads items, then boosts, then conversions, rather than the alphabetical order it fell back to before. Needs MMO Skill Tree 1.6.0 or newer.
