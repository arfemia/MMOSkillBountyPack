Contracts are now always claimed at the Bounty Board, so a finished contract's reward can never be lost. Requires MMO Skill Tree 1.4.3.

- **Fixed: contract rewards are collected at the board, never handed out in the field.** A finished contract now waits at the Bounty Board for you to claim it, instead of dropping the reward on the spot. Paired with MMO Skill Tree 1.4.3's "Ready to turn in" section, a finished contract stays claimable even after it rotates off the board, so a reward can no longer be stranded by a full inventory or by the contract expiring.
- **Requires MMO Skill Tree 1.4.3.** The always-claim-at-the-board behavior relies on engine support added in 1.4.3, so update the mod alongside this pack.
