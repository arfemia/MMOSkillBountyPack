New contracts across every board, a Snapdragon hunt in place of the old Risen contract, and a Snapdragon kill achievement. Requires MMO Skill Tree 1.4.3.

- **New: the Snapdragon hunt.** A tough daily contract to track down the venomous Snapdragon, replacing the old "Lay the Risen" contract.
- **New: five more contracts spread across the boards.** Burnt Skeletons and a wild Boar hunt join the Daily board, Sandswept Skeletons and a frost-bound skeleton host arrive on the Weekly board, and a fast zombie cull rounds out the Quick board.
- **New: two achievement chains.** "Snapdragon Slayer" and "Snapdragon Bane" reward completing the Snapdragon contract, and a "Quick Contractor" ladder (up to "Quick Legend") rewards completing Quick contracts, giving the fast board its own progression alongside the Daily and Weekly ladders.
- **Full 9-language translations** for every new contract and both achievement chains, matching the rest of the pool.
