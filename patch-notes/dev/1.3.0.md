Three seasonal contracts and a seasonal band on the Daily board, built on ZiggfreedCommon 2.2.0's calendar switches and its commerce hide axis. No shipped id changes.

- **Requires ZiggfreedCommon `>=2.2.0`** (manifest `Dependencies`); the MMO pin stays `^1.6.1`, since nothing here needs 1.7.0. Two 2.2.0 pieces carry the contracts. zc-calendar declares the `ziggfreedcommon` feature namespace at library setup and a running switch, `<Event>_Live`, for every calendar event a pack loads. zc-commerce lifts a plain top-level feature condition in a contract's `Requires` onto its hide axis (`BountyAsset.isAvailable()`, read by `BountyAssetRef.enabled()`, which `BoardEngine.membersOf` filters before the seeded draw), so a contract whose switch reads off leaves the draw, and a carried one stays on the board's Mine tab (`BoardEngine.namingBoard`). On 2.1.x the same condition is an ordinary lock and the contracts would post all year, locked.
- **New: `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/Bounties/MMOSkillTree/Haunt/Bounty_Haunt_{Ghouls,Skeletons,Souls}.json`** (ids `bounty_haunt_ghouls`, `bounty_haunt_skeletons`, `bounty_haunt_souls`): children of `Bounty_Kill` (`KILL_ENTITY`, `CONTAINS`) on the Daily board in band `Seasonal`, `Weight` 1. Each gates on `{"Factor": "ziggfreedcommon:feature", "Param": "Hallows_Eve_Live", "Min": 1}` at the top level of `Requires`: 1 while the Hallows_Eve event is switched on and between its dates, 0 when it is forced off, switched off, between seasons, or not loaded at all (a declared namespace answers 0 for a feature nobody declared). The targets are the Hallow's Eve pack's role ids `Hallows_Eve_Hollow_Ghoul`, `Hallows_Eve_Jack_Skeleton` and `Hallows_Eve_Lost_Soul`; a kill is matched on the victim's role name, so the vanilla roles those mobs are built on never count. Pay is `Rewards.Claim`: `bounty_token` and `Hallows_Eve_Sweets` through the `Currency` kind (the wallet resolves when it pays, so the pack loads cleanly without it), plus `Mmo_Xp` in ARTILLERY, BLUNT and STAVES.
- **Changed: `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/Boards/MMOSkillTree/Daily.json`** gains a fifth slot line, `{"Difficulty": "Seasonal", "Optional": true}`, last, and `Grades.Seasonal` (`board.grade.seasonal`); no `AcceptRequires` entry, so the band is open to everyone. The weighted draw fills the slots in order from one seeded `Random`, and a slot with no eligible contract takes no roll, so the trailing slot never moves an earlier position: out of season the board draws exactly as 1.2.1 did, and in season the six regular positions are the same. Keep the slot last in any override. A board override that replaces `Slots` drops it; disabling all three contracts while keeping it is an `UNFILLABLE_SLOT` finding.
- **Lang:** `board.grade.seasonal` and `quest.bounty_haunt_{ghouls,skeletons,souls}.{title,flavor}` in `mmoskilltree.lang`, nine locales.
