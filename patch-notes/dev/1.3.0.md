Three seasonal contracts and a seasonal band on the Daily board, built on ZiggfreedCommon 2.2.0's calendar switches and its commerce hide axis; three contracts whose creatures never spawn switched off, and The Shadow Knight retargeted. No shipped id changes.

- **Requires ZiggfreedCommon `>=2.2.0`** (manifest `Dependencies`); the MMO pin stays `^1.6.1`, since nothing here needs 1.7.0. Two 2.2.0 pieces carry the contracts. zc-calendar declares the `ziggfreedcommon` feature namespace at library setup and a running switch, `<Event>_Live`, for every calendar event a pack loads. zc-commerce lifts a plain top-level feature condition in a contract's `Requires` onto its hide axis (`BountyAsset.isAvailable()`, read by `BountyAssetRef.enabled()`, which `BoardEngine.membersOf` filters before the seeded draw), so a contract whose switch reads off leaves the draw, and a carried one stays on the board's Mine tab (`BoardEngine.namingBoard`). On 2.1.x the same condition is an ordinary lock and the contracts would post all year, locked.
- **New: `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/Bounties/MMOSkillTree/Haunt/Bounty_Haunt_{Ghouls,Skeletons,Souls}.json`** (ids `bounty_haunt_ghouls`, `bounty_haunt_skeletons`, `bounty_haunt_souls`): children of `Bounty_Kill` (`KILL_ENTITY`, `CONTAINS`) on the Daily board in band `Seasonal`, `Weight` 1. Each gates on `{"Factor": "ziggfreedcommon:feature", "Param": "Hallows_Eve_Live", "Min": 1}` at the top level of `Requires`: 1 while the Hallows_Eve event is switched on and between its dates, 0 when it is forced off, switched off, between seasons, or not loaded at all (a declared namespace answers 0 for a feature nobody declared). The targets are the role ids of Seasons of Orbis (`Ziggfreed:SeasonsOfOrbis`, the yearly-events pack whose first event is Hallow's Eve) `Hallows_Eve_Hollow_Ghoul`, `Hallows_Eve_Jack_Skeleton` and `Hallows_Eve_Lost_Soul`; a kill is matched on the victim's role name, so the vanilla roles those mobs are built on never count. Pay is `Rewards.Claim`: `bounty_token` and `Hallows_Eve_Sweets` through the `Currency` kind (the wallet resolves when it pays, so the pack loads cleanly without it), plus `Mmo_Xp` in ARTILLERY, BLUNT and STAVES.
- **Changed: `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/Boards/MMOSkillTree/Daily.json`** gains a fifth slot line, `{"Difficulty": "Seasonal", "Optional": true}`, last, and `Grades.Seasonal` (`board.grade.seasonal`); no `AcceptRequires` entry, so the band is open to everyone. The weighted draw fills the slots in order from one seeded `Random`, and a slot with no eligible contract takes no roll, so the trailing slot never moves an earlier position: out of season the regular positions draw as if the slot were not there, and in season they are the same. Keep the slot last in any override. A board override that replaces `Slots` drops it; disabling all three contracts while keeping it is an `UNFILLABLE_SLOT` finding.
- **Changed: three contracts switched off, one retargeted.** `Bounty_Weekly_Werewolves.json`, `Bounty_Aberrant_Horror.json` and `Bounty_Ghoul_Purge.json` carry `"Enabled": false`: no spawn marker, world spawn, beacon, flock, encounter, prefab or worldgen entry in 0.6.8's `Assets.zip` (nor in Update 7's pre-release) names `Werewolf`, `Zombie_Aberrant` or `Ghoul`, only their own role, model and drop files, and `Ghoul` matched nothing but Seasons of Orbis's `Hallows_Eve_Hollow_Ghoul`. They are switched off rather than deleted so nothing a player holds is stranded: `BoardEngine.membersOf` leaves a disabled contract out of the draw and the reroll pool, while `BoardEngine.namingBoard` keeps a carried or parked one on the board's Mine tab. A deleted definition is skipped by `QuestEngine.activeAndUnclaimed` and left in the store by `selfHeal`, so a parked contract's `Claim` pay could never be collected. Their lang keys stay for that row. A `Weight` of 0 is no switch (read as 1, `NON_POSITIVE_WEIGHT`). The Daily Hard band drops to 8 contracts and the Weekly Hard band to 19, so both draws differ from the period this lands in. `Bounty_Weekly_Shadow_Knight.json` targets `Knight` (`CONTAINS` on the victim's role id, case-insensitive) with `Amount` 15, up from 2; the pay is unchanged (950 `bounty_token`, 9,500 `Mmo_Xp` in STAVES). `Knight` counts the nine skeleton-knight roles, `Skeleton_Knight`, `Skeleton_Burnt_Knight`, `Skeleton_Frost_Knight` and their `_Patrol` and `_Wander` roles, in any mix, plus `Risen_Knight` (the Rekindle Embers spellbook's summoned ally, one consumed book per raise, half of them `Risen_Gunner`), `Shadow_Knight` (no spawn entry on 0.6.8) and `Test_Combat_Knight` (a `Tests` marker only). ZiggfreedCommon 2.2.0 cannot name the nine alone: an objective's `Target` is one string compared under `EXACT`, `CONTAINS` or `PREFIX` (`ObjectiveMatch.targetMatches`), with no list, separator or wildcard, every objective keeps its own `Amount` and the contract completes only when each one does (`QuestEngine.allObjectivesComplete`), and no stem covers the nine without the other three (`Skeleton_` takes every skeleton, `_Knight` all three). Three steps (`Skeleton_Knight`, `Skeleton_Burnt_Knight`, `Skeleton_Frost_Knight`) would exclude them, at the cost of a quota per kind. Its step reads `objective.text.bounty.skeleton_knights`, since no name key matches the bare target. The file and id are unchanged, so saved progress carries over.
- **`Bounty_Swat_Bats.json` matches by `PREFIX`.** Its step's `Target` `Bat` under the parent's `CONTAINS` also matched `Goblin_Scavenger_Battleaxe`; `"MatchMode": "PREFIX"` on the step keeps `Bat` and `Bat_Ice` (no other role starts with Bat).
- **Changed: step lines for targets with no name of their own.** Eighteen contracts carry a step `TextKey`, `objective.text.bounty.{burnt_skeletons,frost_skeletons,sand_skeletons,bears,goblins,scaraks,trorks,wolves,golems,spirits,outlanders,whales,sharks,crops}`: their targets (`Skeleton_Burnt`, `Skeleton_Frost`, `Skeleton_Sand`, `Bear`, `Goblin`, `Scarak`, `Trork`, `Wolf`, `Golem`, `Spirit`, `Outlander`, `Whale`, `Shark`, `Plant_Crop_`) match no `server.npcRoles.<id>.name` or `npcs.<id>.name` key on 0.6.8, so the step fell back to the prettified id in every locale ("Defeat 12 Skeleton Burnt"). The MMO's `ObjectiveTextRenderer` binds `{0}` to the amount as a nested `Msg.num`, so each line spells out its noun, as the Open Warrant and Muster Roll lines do, and none puts a `{0, plural, ...}` selector on that nested message.
- **Changed: en-US text only.** Sixteen contract flavors, three contract titles (`bounty_snapdragon_hunt`, `bounty_weekly_leviathan`, `bounty_daily_bear_hunt`), the Token Shop's offer titles and descriptions, `shop.xp_packet.master.title` and three achievement titles (`bounty_board_weekly_t3`, `bounty_board_bihourly_t2`, `bounty_board_bihourly_t3`) are reworded. No key, file or id is renamed, so contract progress, offer purchase limits and earned achievements carry over.
- **Changed: Essence of Life prices and asks rise by half.** `ShopEntries/MMOSkillTree/Boost_Essence_Surge.json` and `Xp_Packet_All_Skills.json` `Cost.Currencies.Life_Essence` 300 to 450, `Cache_Iron_Bars.json` 60 to 90, `Featured_Cache_Enchanter.json` 80 to 120; `ShopEntryGenerators/MMOSkillTree/Xp_Packets.json` `essence` 30 / 65 / 100 to 45 / 100 / 150 by tier; `Bounties/MMOSkillTree/Bounty_Daily_Essence.json` `Amount` 100 to 150, `Bounty_Deliver_Essence.json` 250 to 375, `Bounty_Market_Patron.json` (the `life_essence` wallet spend) 30 to 45. Token prices, limits and pay are unchanged, except `Boost_Essence_Surge.json`'s `Mmo_Boost_Token` `Multiplier` 2.0 to 2.5 (30 minutes and `Daily` 1 unchanged), so the essence-priced surge outdoes `Boost_All_Skills` (2.0 for 45) in strength. The four lines that named the price in prose (`shop.boost_essence_surge.desc`, `shop.cache_iron_bars.desc`, `shop.featured_cache_enchanter.desc`, `quest.bounty_market_patron.flavor`) say what the offer does instead, in nine locales, so a retune never touches a lang value again.
- **Lang:** `board.grade.seasonal` and `quest.bounty_haunt_{ghouls,skeletons,souls}.{title,flavor}` in `mmoskilltree.lang`, nine locales; `quest.bounty_weekly_shadow_knight.{title,flavor}` reworded (Fallen Knights), `objective.text.bounty.skeleton_knights` and the fourteen step lines above added.
