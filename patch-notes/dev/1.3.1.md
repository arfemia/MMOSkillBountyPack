The bounty pack's Update 7 release, and its first content on ZiggfreedCommon 2.3.0's per-entry board gate and objective `Targets` list. No shipped id changes.

- **Changed: the gathering contracts gate on Mining (M421, R190).** `Bounty_Cache_Copper`, `Bounty_Mine_Iron` and `Bounty_Mine_Silver` (Daily, `Normal`) carry `Boards[0].AcceptRequires` `{"Factors": [{"Factor": "hytale:stat", "Param": "MMO_Level_MINING", "Min": 25}]}`, and `Bounty_Deliver_Adamantite`, `Bounty_Mine_Adamantite` (Daily, `Hard`), `Bounty_Weekly_Mine_Adamantite` and `Bounty_Weekly_Mine_Gold` (Weekly, `Hard`) the same at `Min` 60. zc-commerce's `BoardEngine.entryGateFor` reads an entry's own gate in place of its band's, so the bands' `MMO_CombatLevel` 25 and 60 are not asked of them. Each sits on one board, so each authors a one-entry `Boards` list. The Easy gathering contracts (`Bounty_Cache_Repairs`, `Bounty_Chop_Wood`, `Bounty_Deliver_Iron`, `Bounty_Mine_Stone`) stay ungated, and `Bounty_Deliver_Hides` and `Bounty_Deliver_Essence` keep the Daily `Normal` band's combat 25.
- **Changed: four training contracts open their entry.** `Bounty_Train_Mining` (Daily, `Normal`), `Bounty_Train_Excavation` (Daily, `Hard`), `Bounty_Weekly_Train_Defense` (Weekly, `Normal`) and `Bounty_Weekly_Train_Swords` (Weekly, `Hard`) carry `"AcceptRequires": {}`, an authored empty block that opens the entry whatever the band asks. Their own `Requires` (`hytale:stat` `MMO_Level_<SKILL>` `Min` 1), which 2.2.0's board never read, is checked by `BoardEngine.canAccept` after the entry gate (`BountyAsset.acceptGate()`, after `FeatureLift`); level 1 is every player's floor, so it locks only on a server without the skill. `Bounty_Train_Woodcutting` (Daily, `Easy`) is unchanged. `Bounty_Xp`'s and `Bounty_Train_Mining`'s `$Comment` describe the gate as it now reads.
- **Changed: the bear hunts list three roles (M460, R112).** `Bounty_Bear_Hunt` and `Bounty_Daily_Bear_Hunt` trade the `main` step's `Target` `Bear`, under the `Bounty_Kill` parent's `CONTAINS`, for `"Targets": ["Bear_Grizzly", "Bear_Polar", "Bear_Voidtaken"]` with `"MatchMode": "EXACT"`: `Bear` also matched Update 7's `Bear_Voidtaken_Tentacle` and `Bear_Voidtaken_Tentacle_Marker`. A kill is matched on the victim's role id (`EntityIdentifierUtil.getMobId`), and all three are role files in Update 7's `Assets.zip`. The step line stays `objective.text.bounty.bears`.
- **Changed: Fallen Knights names its nine roles (M443).** `Bounty_Weekly_Shadow_Knight` trades `Target` `Knight` (`CONTAINS`) for `Targets` `Skeleton_Knight`, `Skeleton_Burnt_Knight` and `Skeleton_Frost_Knight` with each one's `_Patrol` and `_Wander` role, under `EXACT`, so `Risen_Knight`, `Shadow_Knight` and `Test_Combat_Knight` stop counting. One step keeps one `Amount` (15) over any mix, and its pay is unchanged. Its `$Comment` is rewritten for the list.
- `Targets` and an entry's `AcceptRequires` are 2.3.0 leaves, which the manifest's `Ziggfreed:ZiggfreedCommon` `>=2.3.0` already asks for.
