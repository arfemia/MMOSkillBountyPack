The bounty pack's Update 7 release, and its first content on ZiggfreedCommon 2.3.0's per-entry board gate and objective `Targets` list. No shipped id changes. `manifest.json` declares `ServerVersion` `>=0.7.0-pre.0 <0.8.0` (was `>=0.6.0-pre.13 <0.7.0`) and the dependencies `Ziggfreed:MMOSkillTree ^1.7.1` and `Ziggfreed:ZiggfreedCommon >=2.3.0` (were `^1.6.1` and `>=2.2.0`).

- **Changed: three hunts retarget to Update 7's roles.** Every new target is a role file in Update 7's `Assets.zip`, and each contract's id, lang keys and `Rewards` are unchanged.
  - `Bounty_Weekly_Goblin_Duke` (Weekly, `Hard`): `Target` `Goblin_Duke` (now under Update 7's `_Deprecated` roles) to `Bear_Voidtaken` with `"MatchMode": "EXACT"`, `Amount` 1, so `Bear_Voidtaken_Tentacle` and `Bear_Voidtaken_Tentacle_Marker` never count under the `Bounty_Kill` parent's `CONTAINS`. `quest.bounty_weekly_goblin_duke.title` reads The Voidtaken Grizzly and `.flavor` names the Guttering Hollows, the spyglass and the Voidheart bait.
  - `Bounty_Smash_Ogres` (Daily, `Hard`): `Target` `Goblin_Ogre`, which Update 7 removes, to `Goblin_Glutton`, the final 0.7.0's overworld elite; the parent's `CONTAINS` also counts `Coffer_Goblin_Glutton` and `Dungeon_Goblin_Glutton`. `Amount` goes from 6 to 3, and a new `Requires` (`ziggfreedcommon:counter` `Bounty_Glutton_Kills` `Min` 1) shows it locked until the player has a Glutton kill of any kind. `quest.bounty_smash_ogres.title` reads Glutton Smash and `.flavor` names the Goblin Gluttons, in all nine locales.
  - `Bounty_Weekly_Voidspawn` (Weekly, `Hard`): `Target` `Spawn_Void` to `Void_Spawn` (`CONTAINS`: `Void_Spawn`, the plain void spawn found outside the Hollows, `Void_Spawn_Entombed`, `Void_Spawn_Entombed_Anomaly`, `Void_Spawn_Surge`), `Amount` 14 to 6, and a step `TextKey` of its own, `objective.text.bounty.voidspawn` ("Defeat {0} Void Fiends", the game's nameplate), in all nine locales.
- **New: two weekly contracts for the final 0.7.0's goblins.**
  - `Bounty_Weekly_Gluttons` (Weekly, `Hard`, a `Bounty_Kill` child): 15 `Goblin_Glutton`, for 700 Bounty Tokens and a combat `Mmo_Lamp` of 8,000. It sits behind the same Glutton tally `Requires` as `Bounty_Smash_Ogres`.
  - `Bounty_Weekly_Feastmaster` (Weekly, `Hard`, a `Bounty_Encounter` child): one `Dungeon_Feastmaster_Encounter` won, with the step line `objective.text.bounty.feastmaster`, for 1,200 Bounty Tokens and a combat `Mmo_Lamp` of 12,000. It sits behind `ziggfreedcommon:achievement_earned` `Goblin_Feastmaster_T1`, MMO Skill Tree's Feastmaster Down.
  - The new `Stats/Bounty_Glutton_Kills.json`, a `Sum` stat counting `KILL_ENTITY` on `Goblin_Glutton` under `CONTAINS`, is the Glutton tally both locks read: CommonLib's `Mob_Kills` keeps one line per exact role, so its `Mob_Kills/Goblin_Glutton` never counted `Dungeon_Goblin_Glutton` or `Coffer_Goblin_Glutton`. On 0.7.0 `CONTAINS` matches only those three roles and the abstract `Template_Goblin_Glutton`, which never spawns. A stat counts from its first load, so there is nothing to migrate. It names no `NameKey`, since no Records section shows it.
  - The new `Factors/Bounty_Goblin_Glutton_Kills.json` names the Glutton tally (`factor.counter.goblin_glutton`) on `Param` `Bounty_Glutton_Kills`, so the lock reads "Requires beating a Goblin Glutton".
  - New keys: `quest.bounty_weekly_gluttons.*`, `quest.bounty_weekly_feastmaster.*`, `objective.text.bounty.feastmaster` and `factor.counter.goblin_glutton`, in all nine locales.
  - `Bounty_Encounter`'s `$Comment` names the rules the library still ships.
- **Changed: the gathering contracts gate on Mining.** `Bounty_Cache_Copper`, `Bounty_Mine_Iron` and `Bounty_Mine_Silver` (Daily, `Normal`) carry `Boards[0].AcceptRequires` `{"Factors": [{"Factor": "hytale:stat", "Param": "MMO_Level_MINING", "Min": 25}]}`, and `Bounty_Deliver_Adamantite`, `Bounty_Mine_Adamantite` (Daily, `Hard`), `Bounty_Weekly_Mine_Adamantite` and `Bounty_Weekly_Mine_Gold` (Weekly, `Hard`) the same at `Min` 60. zc-commerce's `BoardEngine.entryGateFor` reads an entry's own gate in place of its band's, so the bands' `MMO_CombatLevel` 25 and 60 are not asked of them. Each sits on one board, so each authors a one-entry `Boards` list. The Easy gathering contracts (`Bounty_Cache_Repairs`, `Bounty_Chop_Wood`, `Bounty_Deliver_Iron`, `Bounty_Mine_Stone`) stay ungated, and `Bounty_Deliver_Hides` and `Bounty_Deliver_Essence` keep the Daily `Normal` band's combat 25.
- **Changed: four training contracts open their entry.** `Bounty_Train_Mining` (Daily, `Normal`), `Bounty_Train_Excavation` (Daily, `Hard`), `Bounty_Weekly_Train_Defense` (Weekly, `Normal`) and `Bounty_Weekly_Train_Swords` (Weekly, `Hard`) carry `"AcceptRequires": {}`, an authored empty block that opens the entry whatever the band asks. Their own `Requires` (`hytale:stat` `MMO_Level_<SKILL>` `Min` 1), which 2.2.0's board never read, is checked by `BoardEngine.canAccept` after the entry gate (`BountyAsset.acceptGate()`, after `FeatureLift`); level 1 is every player's floor, so it locks only on a server without the skill. `Bounty_Train_Woodcutting` (Daily, `Easy`) is unchanged. `Bounty_Xp`'s and `Bounty_Train_Mining`'s `$Comment` describe the gate as it now reads.
- **Changed: the bear hunts list three roles.** `Bounty_Bear_Hunt` and `Bounty_Daily_Bear_Hunt` trade the `main` step's `Target` `Bear`, under the `Bounty_Kill` parent's `CONTAINS`, for `"Targets": ["Bear_Grizzly", "Bear_Polar", "Bear_Voidtaken"]` with `"MatchMode": "EXACT"`: `Bear` also matched Update 7's `Bear_Voidtaken_Tentacle` and `Bear_Voidtaken_Tentacle_Marker`. A kill is matched on the victim's role id (`EntityIdentifierUtil.getMobId`), and all three are role files in Update 7's `Assets.zip`. The step line stays `objective.text.bounty.bears`.
- **Changed: the goblin culls count the Feastmaster's Scrappers.** `Bounty_Goblin_Cleanup` (Daily, `Normal`) and `Bounty_Weekly_Goblin_Purge` (Weekly, `Normal`) keep `Target` `Goblin` under the parent's `CONTAINS` and add `"Targets": ["Dungeon_Feastmaster_Scrapper"]`, the adds of the Feastmaster's fight, whose role id carries no `Goblin`. Amounts, pay and the step line `objective.text.bounty.goblins` are unchanged.
- **Changed: Fallen Knights names its nine roles.** `Bounty_Weekly_Shadow_Knight` trades `Target` `Knight` (`CONTAINS`) for `Targets` `Skeleton_Knight`, `Skeleton_Burnt_Knight` and `Skeleton_Frost_Knight` with each one's `_Patrol` and `_Wander` role, under `EXACT`, so `Risen_Knight`, `Shadow_Knight` and `Test_Combat_Knight` stop counting. One step keeps one `Amount` (15) over any mix, and its pay is unchanged. Its `$Comment` is rewritten for the list.
- **Changed: the kill contracts' weapon-skill XP becomes a combat Skill Lamp.** The 48 `Bounty_Kill` children whose `Rewards.Claim` paid `Mmo_Xp` in one weapon skill (Swords, Daggers, Polearms, Staves, Axes, Blunt, Archery, Magic, Artillery or Unarmed) pay `{"Kind": "Mmo_Lamp", "Params": {"Amount": X, "Picks": 1, "Skills": "COMBAT"}}` in that row, X unchanged: one pick from the `COMBAT` category, which also holds Defense and Acrobatics. The `Currency` rows and the Defense and Acrobatics rows (`Bounty_Clear_Spiders`, `Bounty_Daily_Monster_Cull`, `Bounty_Quick_Cull`, `Bounty_Weekly_Feran_Pack`) are unchanged, as are `Bounty_Deliver_Hides` (a `TURN_IN`) and `Bounty_Weekly_Train_Swords` (a `GAIN_XP` goal), which are not kill contracts. `Mmo_Lamp` is MMO Skill Tree 1.7.1's kind, which the manifest's `Ziggfreed:MMOSkillTree` `^1.7.1` already asks for.
- `Targets` and an entry's `AcceptRequires` are 2.3.0 leaves, which the manifest's `Ziggfreed:ZiggfreedCommon` `>=2.3.0` already asks for.
- **Removed: fourteen `ui.hub.*` keys, in all nine locales.** Six (`ui.hub.subtitle`, `ui.hub.shop_button`, `ui.hub.tutorial.step`, `.next`, `.back`, `.finish`) repeated the MMO jar's own keys with the same values, and eight (`ui.hub.bounties_button`, `ui.hub.bounties_hint` and the tutorial's `welcome`, `bounties` and `shop` titles and bodies) were read by nothing, the onboarding topics reading `ui.onboarding.*`. Nothing a player sees changes.
