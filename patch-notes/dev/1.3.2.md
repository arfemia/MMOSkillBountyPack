A small release beside ZiggfreedCommon 2.4.0's board reading. No shipped id changes. `manifest.json` changes only its `Version`: the dependencies stay `Ziggfreed:MMOSkillTree ^1.7.1` and `Ziggfreed:ZiggfreedCommon >=2.3.0`, since a band's `Color` under a board's `Grades` is a 2.3.0 leaf.

- **Changed: each board colours its own grades.** `Boards/MMOSkillTree/Daily.json` authors `Grades.<band>.Color` for all five bands it posts (`Training` `#43d9c4`, `Easy` `#4aff7f`, `Normal` `#4a9eff`, `Hard` `#ffaa4a`, and `Seasonal` `#ff9f43` beside its existing `TitleKey`), `Weekly.json` for `Normal` and `Hard`, and `Bihourly.json` for `Easy`: the colours MMO Skill Tree's `MmoBounties.difficultyColor` painted before ZiggfreedCommon 2.4.0's default board reading took over the grade pill. A band a board does not post gets no colour and reads on a plain pill. Each board's `$Comment` says what `Grades` colours.
- **Changed: Voidspawn Incursion's flavor.** en-US `quest.bounty_weekly_voidspawn.flavor` reads "The void is coming through the rift again." where it read "bleeding through the rift", and the other eight locales are retranslated to match.
