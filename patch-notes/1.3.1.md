New weekly contracts send you after Update 7's Voidtaken Grizzly, Goblin Gluttons and the Feastmaster, and the mining contracts now ask for your Mining level.

Built for Update 7: it needs MMO Skill Tree 1.7.1 and Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.3.0 or newer, and it won't load on Update 6, so update them together.

### New

- Added The Feastmaster, a hard weekly boss contract: win one fight against the Feastmaster, deep in the goblin Deepworks, for 1,200 Bounty Tokens and a Skill Lamp. It unlocks once you earn Feastmaster Down, MMO Skill Tree's achievement for your first win against it. You reach the Deepworks through an Ancient Gateway with a Deepworks fragment, crafted at the Goblin Tinkerer's Workbench in the goblin lab beyond the Forgotten Temple.
- Added Glutton Cull, a hard weekly hunt for 15 Goblin Gluttons, for 700 Bounty Tokens and a Skill Lamp. It unlocks once you bring down your first Glutton.

### Changes

- The weekly boss hunt moves from the Goblin Duke, gone in Update 7, to the Voidtaken Grizzly. Spot one in the Guttering Hollows with a spyglass, bait the bear trap there with a Voidheart and bring it down. One kill settles it, for the same pay.
- Mining contracts ask for your Mining level instead of your combat level. Copper Cache, Iron for the Forge and Silver Consignment want Mining 25, and Adamantite Run, Adamantite Consignment, Adamantite Quota and Gold Quota want Mining 60. Quarry Work, Timber, Tools of the Trade and Iron Delivery stay open to everyone, and Hide Haul and Essence Consignment still ask for combat level 25.
- The hard daily Ogre Smash is now Glutton Smash, since Update 7 removes the Goblin Ogre. It sends you after 3 Goblin Gluttons, the heavy goblins out in the world (Coffer and Deepworks Gluttons count too), for the same pay, and unlocks once you bring down your first Glutton of any kind.
- Drill the Pick, Dig Deep, Hold the Line and Sword Mastery no longer ask for a combat level, so you can take them from the start, like Sharpen the Axe.
- Hunting contracts that gave XP in one weapon skill now give a Skill Lamp worth the same XP. Rub it and pick the combat skill that gets it. Their Bounty Tokens are unchanged.
- Voidspawn Incursion asks for 6 of Update 7's Void Fiends where it asked for 14, for the same pay.
- Bear Hunt and Bears in the Stores count the Grizzly Bear, the Polar Bear and the Voidtaken Grizzly.
- Goblin Cleanup and Goblin Purge count the Scrappers the Feastmaster calls into its fight, along with every other goblin.

### Fixes

- Fixed an issue where Fallen Knights counted the Risen Knights a spellbook raises. It counts Skeleton Knights, Burnt Skeleton Knights and Frost Skeleton Knights, patrolling and wandering ones included, in any mix.
