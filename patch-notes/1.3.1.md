This one moves the bounty boards onto Hytale Update 7. A weekly boss hunt goes after Update 7's Voidtaken Grizzly, and the mining contracts ask for your Mining level. It needs an Update 7 server, MMO Skill Tree 1.7.1 and Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.3.0 or newer, and it won't load on Update 6, so update them together.

### Update 7

- **The Voidtaken Grizzly takes over from the Goblin Duke.** Update 7 retires the Goblin Duke, so the weekly boss hunt The Goblin Duke is now The Voidtaken Grizzly. Track one down in the Guttering Hollows with a spyglass, bait the bear trap there with a Voidheart, and bring it down. One kill settles the contract, for the same pay.
- **Goblin Feastmasters take over from the ogres.** Update 7 removes the Goblin Ogre, so the hard daily Ogre Smash is now Feastmaster Smash and sends you after 6 Goblin Feastmasters, Coffer Feastmasters included, for the same pay.
- **Voidspawn Incursion hunts Void Fiends.** The weekly counts Update 7's Void Fiends and asks for 6 where it asked for 14, for the same pay. Its step reads "Defeat 6 Void Fiends".
- **The bear hunts count only bears.** Bear Hunt and Bears in the Stores count the Grizzly Bear, the Polar Bear and the Voidtaken Grizzly, and never the tentacles the Voidtaken Grizzly calls up.

### Changes

- **Mining contracts ask for your Mining level.** Copper Cache, Iron for the Forge and Silver Consignment want Mining 25 where they wanted combat level 25, and Adamantite Run, Adamantite Consignment, Adamantite Quota and Gold Quota want Mining 60 where they wanted combat level 60. Quarry Work, Timber, Tools of the Trade and Iron Delivery stay open to everyone, and Hide Haul and Essence Consignment still ask for combat level 25.
- **The training contracts are open to everyone.** Drill the Pick, Dig Deep, Hold the Line and Sword Mastery no longer ask for a combat level, so you can take them from the start, as you already could Sharpen the Axe.
- **Hunting contracts give a Skill Lamp where they gave experience in one weapon.** Rub the lamp and choose the combat skill that gets the experience, so it goes to the weapon you fight with. The Bounty Tokens they pay are the same.

### Fixes

- **Fixed: Fallen Knights counted the Risen Knights a spellbook raises.** It counts Skeleton Knights, Burnt Skeleton Knights and Frost Skeleton Knights, the ones on patrol or wandering included, in any mix, and no other knight.
