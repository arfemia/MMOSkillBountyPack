Contract grades keep the colours you know on CommonLib 2.4.0's quest page.

Built for Update 7: it needs MMO Skill Tree 1.7.1 and Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.3.0 or newer. With MMO Skill Tree 1.7.2 and CommonLib 2.4.0, the boards open on the quest page.

### Changes

- The Daily, Weekly and Quick Contracts boards now set the colour of each grade they post, the same colours as before, so a contract's grade pill keeps its colour on CommonLib 2.4.0's quest page and in your quest book, where a board without them shows a plain pill.
- Voidspawn Incursion's flavor text now says the void is coming through the rift, in every language.
