Haunt contracts for Hallow's Eve, the first event in the Seasons of Orbis pack. While the event runs, the Daily board posts one more contract each day, sending you after the creatures that come out at night. It goes in a Seasonal slot of its own at the end of the board, so it never takes a regular contract's place. Outside the event, and on any server without Seasons of Orbis, the slot stays empty.

- **Requires Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.2.0 or newer, beside MMO Skill Tree 1.6.1 or newer.** The haunt contracts follow the event calendar that arrives in 2.2.0, and the 2.2.0 board is the one that leaves them off while the event is over. Update the library jar and this pack together.
- **New: three haunt contracts.** Ghoul Season, Pumpkin Heads and Wandering Souls send you after the Hollow Ghouls, Jack Skeletons and Lost Souls of Hallow's Eve. Finish one and collect at the board: Bounty Tokens and skill experience, with a few Hallow Sweets on top, and each one you finish counts toward the Daily board's achievement ladder.
- **New: a Seasonal slot on the Daily board.** It asks for no combat level, so anyone can take the day's haunt contract, and a reroll swaps it for another one.
- **The regular skeleton contracts count the event's Jack Skeletons too.** During Hallow's Eve, a Jack Skeleton counts toward a plain skeleton contract (the burnt, frost and sand skeleton contracts still want their own kind).
- **A haunt contract you are still carrying when the event ends is not lost.** It stays on the board's Mine tab, where you can hand it in and collect, or give it up.
- **Plainer contract text.** About thirty-five contracts read more plainly, and five contracts take new names: Prickly Problem becomes Walking Cactee, Trillodon Terror becomes The Trillodon, The Snapdragon becomes Snapdragon Hunt, The Leviathan becomes Whale Hunt, and the training Bear Hunt becomes Bears in the Stores, so it no longer shares a name with the hard one. What each contract asks and pays is unchanged.
- **The Token Shop says what it sells.** Each offer's line names what you get. The featured crates drop their "Featured:" prefix for names of their own (Large Iron Crate, Adamantite Stash, Fisher's Haul and the rest), the Hunter's Hide Bundle becomes the Scaled Hide Bundle, and the XP Exchange's biggest packet is an Apprenticeship, since Mastery is already the name of another system.
- **The offers that take Essence of Life ask for half again as much.** The XP packets want 45, 100 and 150 by size, the All-Skills XP Bundle and Essence Surge 450, Smith's Reserve 90 and the Enchanter's Parcel 120, beside the same tokens as before. The three contracts built around essence follow: Daily Essence asks for 150, Deliver Essence for 375, and Market Patron counts 45 spent at the counter. Their pay is unchanged. Essence Surge earns its price: it lifts every skill by two and a half times for its half hour, where it matched the All-Skills Surge's double.
- **Three achievements take new names.** Weekly Legend becomes Warrant Veteran, and the quick-contract ladder's Rapid Responder and Quick Legend become Odd-Job Regular and Odd-Job Veteran. Progress and anything you already earned carry over.
- **Fixed: contract steps that showed a raw creature id.** "Defeat 12 Skeleton Burnt" reads "Defeat 12 Burnt Skeletons", and the other hunts whose creature the game has no single name for (bears, goblins, trorks, wolves and more), plus the daily harvest, read in plain words that each language can translate.
- **Fixed: four contracts asked for creatures the game never spawns.** Under the Full Moon, Aberrant Horror and Ghoul Purge are off the boards, so they no longer cost you a reroll to clear. One you already took stays on the board's Mine tab, where you can still hand it in or give it up. The Shadow Knight becomes Fallen Knights and counts the skeleton knights that do show up: it asks for 15 now, up from 2, for the same pay. Skeleton Knights, Burnt Skeleton Knights and Frost Skeleton Knights all count, in any mix.

- **Fixed: Swat the Bats counted a goblin.** The contract matched any creature with "bat" in its name, which took in the goblin scavenger's battleaxe. It counts bats and ice bats now, nothing else.
