Haunt contracts for Hallow's Eve, the first event in the Seasons of Orbis pack. While the event runs, the Daily board posts one more contract each day, sending you after the creatures that come out at night. It goes in a Seasonal slot of its own at the end of the board, so the regular contracts stay as they were. Outside the event, and on any server without Seasons of Orbis, the slot stays empty.

- **Requires Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.2.0 or newer, beside MMO Skill Tree 1.6.1 or newer.** The haunt contracts follow the event calendar that arrives in 2.2.0, and the 2.2.0 board is the one that leaves them off while the event is over. Update the library jar and this pack together.
- **New: three haunt contracts.** Ghoul Season, Pumpkin Heads and Wandering Souls send you after the Hollow Ghouls, Jack Skeletons and Lost Souls of Hallow's Eve. Claim one at the board for Bounty Tokens and skill experience, with a few Hallow Sweets on top, and each one you finish counts toward the Daily board's achievement ladder.
- **New: a Seasonal slot on the Daily board.** It asks for no combat level, so anyone can take the day's haunt contract, and a reroll swaps it for another one.
- **The regular ghoul and skeleton contracts count the event's creatures too.** During Hallow's Eve, a Hollow Ghoul counts toward a ghoul contract and a Jack Skeleton toward a plain skeleton one (the burnt, frost and sand skeleton contracts still want their own kind).
- **A haunt contract you are still carrying when the event ends is not lost.** It stays on the board's Mine tab, where you can hand it in and claim it, or give it up.
- **Plainer contract text.** About twenty contracts and the Bounty Master's welcome read more plainly, and two contracts take new names: Prickly Problem becomes Walking Cactee, and Trillodon Terror becomes The Trillodon. What each contract asks and pays is unchanged.
