First release: rotating bounty contracts, Bounty Tokens, and the shops to spend them in.

- **Daily and Weekly Bounty Boards.** Kill, gather, train-a-skill, and delivery (turn-in) contracts that refresh on a shared rotation; accept what you want, finish anywhere, auto-claim on completion. Harder contracts gate behind a minimum combat level.
- **Per-contract rerolls.** Swap a single contract you don't want for a fresh one (the rest of the board stays), up to a daily cap, paid in Bounty Tokens.
- **Two shop storefronts.** A Token Trader block (or NPC) opens the shop: the general **Token Shop** (XP boosts, item caches, currency conversions, a rotating Featured shelf with paid rerolls and a starter copper crate) and a per-skill **XP Exchange** covering every skill on the server.
- **Placeable blocks + spawn integration.** Craft and mount Daily/Weekly board and Token Trader blocks anywhere; the mod's Adventurer's Guide NPC at world spawn lists the boards and shops with no setup (opt out in `spawn-hub.json`).
- **Bounty achievements.** Chains for completing contracts, including per-board Daily and Weekly ladders and daily streaks.
- **Fully translated.** Every contract, board, shop offer, block, and NPC name ships in 9 languages; missing keys fall back to English per key.
