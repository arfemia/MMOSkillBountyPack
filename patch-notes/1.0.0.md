First release: rotating bounty contracts, Bounty Tokens, and the shops to spend them in.

- **Daily and Weekly Bounty Boards.** Kill, gather, mine-and-deliver, train-a-skill, and turn-in contracts that refresh on a shared rotation; accept what you want, finish anywhere, auto-claim on completion. Harder contracts gate behind a minimum combat level.
- **A world full of targets.** Hunt across the bestiary, from desert scorpions, cave raptors, and sabertooths to swamp crocodiles, fen stalkers, mountain yetis, ghouls, restless spirits, and voidspawn.
- **Marquee boss hunts.** A handful of standout, hard-tier contracts send you after the Goblin Duke, the Shadow Knight, the Scarak Broodmother, and an aberrant horror, for a bigger payout than a regular hard contract.
- **Mine and deliver.** Many gathering contracts now ask you to hand a share of your haul back at the board, not just break blocks, so a mining run ends with a delivery.
- **Per-contract rerolls.** Swap a single contract you don't want for a fresh one (the rest of the board stays), up to a daily cap, paid in Bounty Tokens.
- **Two shop storefronts.** A Token Trader block (or NPC) opens the shop: the general **Token Shop** (XP boosts, item caches, currency conversions, a rotating Featured shelf with paid rerolls and a starter copper crate, plus premium offers paid in tokens AND Life Essence) and the **XP Exchange**, whose daily "Today's XP Deals" slate rotates one Lesser, one Greater, and one Master XP packet (each for a different randomly featured skill, rerollable) over an always-available All-Skills XP Bundle.
- **Placeable blocks + spawn integration.** Craft and mount Daily/Weekly board and Token Trader blocks anywhere; the mod's Adventurer's Guide NPC at world spawn lists the boards and shops with no setup (opt out in `spawn-hub.json`).
- **Bounty achievements.** Chains for completing contracts, including per-board Daily and Weekly ladders and daily streaks.
- **Fully translated.** Every contract, board, shop offer, block, and NPC name ships in 9 languages; missing keys fall back to English per key.
