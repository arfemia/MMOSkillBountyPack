# MMO Skill Bounty Pack

Adds bounty boards to [MMO Skill Tree](https://www.curseforge.com/hytale/mods/mmo-skill-tree): daily and weekly contracts to hunt, mine and gather for Bounty Tokens, Skill Lamps and XP.

A free content pack. It needs MMO Skill Tree 1.7.1 or newer and Ziggfreed's CommonLib 2.3.0 or newer, on a Hytale Update 7 server.

## What it adds

- Three boards with 80 contracts between them. The Daily board posts a fresh mix every day: hunts for desert scorpions, cave raptors, swamp crocodiles and sabertooths, ore runs, deliveries and skill training.
- The Weekly board pays the most. Its big hunts send you after the Voidtaken Grizzly, the Scarak Broodmother and the Feastmaster, the boss of the goblin Deepworks. An open warrant pays everyone who helps bring down any boss fight on your server.
- Quick Contracts, a board with one small job that changes every two hours, for skill XP or a Skill Lamp.
- **Bounty Tokens**, the currency contracts pay. Spend them at the Token Shop on XP boosts and item crates, or at the XP Exchange on skill XP.
- Hunting contracts also give a Skill Lamp. Rub it and pick the combat skill that gets the XP.
- Achievements for each board, and a Snapdragon hunting chain.
- With [Seasons of Orbis](https://www.curseforge.com/hytale/mods/seasons-of-orbis), the Daily board posts a haunt contract each day of Hallow's Eve, with a few Hallow Sweets on top.
- Translated into 9 languages.

## How it works

1. Find the Adventurer's Guide. He stands beside your world's spawn point and in the Forgotten Temple. Press F, open the menu and pick a board. With MMO Skill Tree 1.7.2 and CommonLib 2.4.0 it opens on the quest page, with the board's wallets at the top and a countdown to its next contracts.
2. Take the contracts you want. One you can't take yet still shows, locked, with what it still needs.
3. Do the work anywhere in the world.
4. Go back to the board and press Collect. Your reward waits there, so a full inventory or a new rotation never costs you one.
5. Don't like a contract? Reroll it for a few Bounty Tokens, a few times a day.

Everyone on the server sees the same contracts each rotation.

## For server owners

- Nothing to set up. The Adventurer's Guide appears at spawn the first time someone joins, and he never doubles up on a restart.
- To put boards somewhere else, give yourself the Bounty Board, Weekly Bounty Board, Quick Contracts Board and Token Trader blocks and place them on any wall.
- Don't want the guide at spawn? Run `/mmonpc disable --arg1=mmo_hub`, or set `"enabled": false` for `mmo_hub` in `mods/ziggfreedcommon/npc-placements.json` before anyone joins. The blocks keep working without him.
- Bounties switch on or off from the in-game admin menu.
- Contracts, boards and shop offers are plain files in the pack. Drop in a file with the same name to change a number, or a new one to add a contract. The pack's repository has a short guide.
